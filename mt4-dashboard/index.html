Load your MT4 collector files

This page reads the CSV files written by MT4Collector_Quant and computes everything in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Click Choose folder and pick a folder that contains the collector files, for example C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\MetaQuotes\Terminal (all terminals at once) or your Google Drive folder that holds the terminals' Reports folders.
  2. The page searches every subfolder for: *_DetailedStatementLive.csv (closed trades), *_open_now.csv (open positions), *_account_snapshots.csv (balance / equity) and *_collector_status.txt.
  3. Duplicates across collector versions (QuantV2, V204, V205, V206 ...) are merged by account + ticket.

Tip: AppData is hidden in Windows. Paste %APPDATA%\MetaQuotes\Terminal into the folder dialog's address bar.
